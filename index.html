Nixpkgs CUDA

Nixpkgs CUDA Team aims to deliver first-class support for [CUDA-]accelerated computing in Nixpkgs and the Ecosystem.

Nixpkgs are a foundational tool promising to save the modern software supply chain from its exploding complexity. Over the past decade "Functional Package Management", once seen as an Academic fancy, has been embraced by the Industry as a requirement for mission-critical systems.

The Nixpkgs CUDA effort began in ambition to revision Reproducible Research, Scientific and High-Performance Computing (HPC) as grounded in these "immutable" and "functional" software management models.

Not CUDA for the sake of CUDA

We believe the "Functional Software Deployment" model of Nix is to become the standard for Research and HPC, just as for the general software industry. We believe this development will foster creativity, and enable the, now forgotten, "playful exploration" for the researcher, the curious "hacker", and the end-user alike. We wish to bring that future closer, so we defend that Nixpkgs accomodate CUDA's needs.

Nixpkgs doesn't need CUDA. Nixpkgs suffers from CUDA, which imposes a heavy maintenance toll on maintainers of stdenv, toolchains, setup-hooks, python3XPackages, and on the general reviewer. Some maintainers would see CUDA removed.

As of $(date), Nixpkgs CUDA does not yet benefit the researcher, the hacker, or the end-user, but it still does impose a heavy toll on other Nixpkgs maintainers. Unintentionally, it does drive the multi-billion dollar enterprise.

Our incentive is to collaborate with the researcher, the RSE, and the individual. For the enterprise, we and the broader Nixpkgs maintainer community offer plentiful [commercial support](#call-for-sponsors) options.
